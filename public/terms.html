How iSpry membership works.

These terms cover the iSpry.ai website and the membership offered by William B. Merrick, DO, PLLC. The payment agreement you accept in Hint controls billing.

Effective October 12, 2026

Membership

  • $100 per month.
  • Up to 3 visits per month.
  • Up to 3 problems addressed per visit.
  • Adults 18 and older who are physically in Tennessee at the time of the visit.

Enrollment and payment happen in Hint. Fast-track pathways on this site run a safety screen, an iSpry account (not membership), Spry education on the problem you chose, then Become a Member — which files intake to Akute before Hint payment. Canceling or changing the plan is done in Hint, under the agreement shown there at signup.

iSpry account

An iSpry account is required before Ask Spry and after a qualifying fast-track screen. It is not membership and does not charge you. You provide name, email, age, sex, and Tennessee location so care rules can be applied and education can continue in your account.

Care

Spry prepares education and intake. William B. Merrick, DO, or a covering clinician, reviews it and decides the plan. Spry does not diagnose or prescribe. iSpry does not prescribe controlled substances. A visit is by telehealth. You confirm telehealth consent before your details are sent to the medical record. After membership payment, the physician review and any prescription follow.

Emergencies

iSpry is not for emergencies. Call 911 for a medical emergency. Call or text 988 if you are in crisis. Do not wait for a chat reply.

The website

The site is the front door to the practice. Information on it can change as the membership and clinical rules change. Using Ask Spry means you agree to keep identifiers out of the chat and to the rules above.
