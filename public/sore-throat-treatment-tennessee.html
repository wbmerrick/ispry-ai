Sore throat

Sore throat treatment in Tennessee.

A sore throat since last night should not require a waiting room. Adults physically in Tennessee can tell Spry what it feels like, then a physician reviews the plan.

The one-time visit is $49 and does not renew. Membership is $100 a month if you want ongoing care. Same-day review applies when the case is in by 6 pm Central.

Go in person, or call 911, if

  • You cannot swallow fluids, you are drooling, or your breathing feels tight.
  • You have a muffled voice, a stiff neck, or you are much sicker than a typical sore throat.
  • You are under 18, or you are not in Tennessee right now.

Call or text (731) 415-0724. Tennessee license 4267.
