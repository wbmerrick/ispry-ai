For employers

Telehealth for your crew.

A sick employee can lose half a day driving to urgent care. iSpry lets adults who are physically in Tennessee start a visit from a phone, with a Tennessee-licensed physician reviewing every case.

What employees get

  • The same iSpry membership sold on this site: up to 3 visits a month and up to 3 problems per visit.
  • Spry intake at any hour. Physician review the same day when submitted by 6 pm Central.
  • A physician-reviewed plan, with prescriptions sent to their own pharmacy when appropriate.

What to know first

  • iSpry is direct-pay care. It is not health insurance and does not replace it.
  • Adults 18 and older who are in Tennessee at the time of the visit. No Medicare enrollment.
  • No controlled substances, and not for emergencies.
  • Prescriptions, labs, and pharmacy charges are separate.
  • Your employees' health information stays between them and the physician. Employers do not receive visit details.

Pricing and setup

For employer pricing, call or text (731) 415-0724 and Dr. Merrick's office will set it up with you. Ask your benefits or tax adviser how a direct-pay membership fits with your health plan and any HSA.
