Weight loss

Physician-led weight loss in Tennessee.

Losing weight and keeping it off is a medical problem, not a willpower test. Adults who are physically in Tennessee can start with Spry, then Dr. Merrick reviews the case and decides the plan.

Spry asks about your height and weight, what you have tried, your health history, and the medicines you take. A one-time visit is $49, or $100 a month covers up to 3 visits, which fits the follow-up that weight-loss care needs.

About GLP-1 medicines

GLP-1 medicines such as semaglutide and tirzepatide are one option. A physician decides whether one is appropriate and safe for you. Spry does not, and a prescription is never guaranteed. The medicine is filled at a pharmacy and billed separately from the visit, and it is usually the largest cost. Insurance coverage varies by plan.

Tell Spry if any of these apply

  • You or a close relative has had medullary thyroid cancer or MEN2.
  • You have had pancreatitis, gallbladder disease, or serious stomach or bowel problems.
  • You are pregnant, planning a pregnancy, or breastfeeding.
  • You take insulin or other diabetes medicines.
  • You have a history of an eating disorder. In-person care with your own clinician is the better place to start.

When a chat is the wrong place

  • Severe belly pain, vomiting that will not stop, or signs of dehydration while on one of these medicines needs in-person care today.
  • Trouble breathing, chest pain, or feeling faint is an emergency. Call 911.
  • iSpry does not see children, does not enroll Medicare beneficiaries, and you must be in Tennessee at the time of the visit.
