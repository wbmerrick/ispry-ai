About iSpry

iSpry is a physician-reviewed telehealth practice for adults who are physically in Tennessee. Spry, an AI assistant, takes the history. A Tennessee-licensed physician (DO) reviews every plan.

AI intake only · physician review 24/7 · Tennessee

How it works

  1. Create a free iSpry account. That is not membership and it does not charge you.
  2. Tell Spry what is going on. Spry asks a short history and screens for emergencies.
  3. A summary of what you told Spry is filed for your iSpry physician. Spry does not diagnose or prescribe.
  4. Your iSpry physician reviews the summary and decides the plan. A video visit is used when the physician needs it. This is not a live waiting room.

Who reviews

Your iSpry physician is a Tennessee-licensed physician (DO). The physician reviews requests 24/7, including nights, weekends, and holidays. Spry does not diagnose or prescribe, and nothing is prescribed automatically.

You are not entering a queue of anonymous network clinicians. The same practice reviews the case. The treating physician's name and Tennessee license are available from the practice on request.

Licensure

Care is only for adults who are physically in Tennessee at the time of the visit. The reviewing physician is licensed in Tennessee. iSpry does not prescribe controlled substances.
