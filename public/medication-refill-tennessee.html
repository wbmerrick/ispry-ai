Medication refill

Online medication refills in Tennessee.

Out of refills and cannot get in with your regular office. Adults who are physically in Tennessee can start with Spry, then Dr. Merrick reviews the request.

Have the medicine name, dose, and your pharmacy ready. A one-time visit is $49, or $100 a month covers up to 3 visits. A physician decides whether a refill is safe and for how long, and may ask for recent labs or a visit with your regular clinician first. Pharmacy charges are separate from the visit.

When a chat is the wrong place

  • iSpry does not prescribe controlled substances. That includes opioids, benzodiazepines, and stimulants.
  • If you are completely out of a medicine you should not miss, such as insulin, a seizure medicine, or a blood thinner, call your pharmacy or go to urgent care today instead of waiting on a chat.
  • Trouble breathing, chest pain, or feeling faint is an emergency. Call 911.
  • iSpry does not see children, and you must be in Tennessee at the time of the visit.
