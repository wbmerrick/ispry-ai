How a case works

Four steps. You own the one that matters.

STEP 1 · SPRY

Safety first

Every message is screened for emergencies. Anything urgent is sent to 911 or 988 before it ever reaches you.

STEP 2 · SPRY

Focused history

A short, condition-specific intake: onset, severity, meds, allergies, and contraindications.

STEP 3 · SPRY

Case summary

A structured synopsis lands in the chart (Akute EHR), ready for your review.

STEP 4 · YOU

Your decision

Diagnose, prescribe, message, request a video visit, or refer to in-person care. Spry never diagnoses or prescribes on its own.

Who we want

Founding physicians who want to build what's next.

You have

  • An MD or DO with an active, unrestricted license
  • Board certification or eligibility in internal medicine, family medicine, or emergency medicine
  • Comfort with asynchronous and video-based care
  • Your own malpractice coverage, or willingness to get a telehealth policy
  • A clean record and an interest in doing medicine differently

We don't do

  • Controlled substances: no opioids, benzodiazepines, or stimulants
  • Emergencies: those go to 911, every time
  • Rubber-stamping: you can decline, ask for more, or send anyone to in-person care
  • Visit quotas or productivity pressure

Questions

What physicians ask first.

Which states are you hiring in?

iSpry sees patients in Tennessee today and is expanding state by state. Tell us every state you're licensed in. Physicians with licenses in our next states will be first in line.

How much does it pay?

Clinicians are paid per completed case. We'll share current rates on an intro call; they depend on state and case mix.

Am I an employee?

No. Clinicians work as independent contractors with the practice and set their own availability.

Who's responsible for the medical decision?

You are, for the cases you review. Spry gathers information and drafts a summary. It never diagnoses, prescribes, or sends anything to a patient as a medical decision.

What tools will I use?

Cases, charting, and e-prescribing run in Akute Health, a HIPAA-compliant EHR. You'll need a computer or tablet and a reliable connection.

Can NPs and PAs apply?

Yes, we're collecting interest. Scope and supervision rules vary by state, so we'll follow up on what's possible where you practice.

iSpry is not for emergencies. If you think you may have a medical emergency, call 911. In crisis? Call or text 988. Spry is an AI intake assistant; it does not diagnose or prescribe. A licensed physician makes all medical decisions.
