Privacy

What this website does with your information.

iSpry.ai is the website for William B. Merrick, DO, PLLC. Health information in your chart is covered by the Notice of Privacy Practices.

Effective October 12, 2026

iSpry account

Before you use Ask Spry (or after you clear a fast-track safety screen), you start an iSpry account — not membership yet. That account collects your name, email, password, age, sex, and whether you are physically in Tennessee. Sign-in uses your email and password (or an email link if you prefer). Passwords are handled by the auth service and are not stored in this browser. Account profile data is stored so you can return; Spry chat text is not kept as a chart.

Spry chat

When you talk with Spry, the text of that conversation is sent to an AI service so Spry can reply. iSpry does not keep a database of those chats. Leave dates of birth and other identifiers out of the chat when possible. Name and contact for the chart belong on the Akute form when you become a member or finish intake.

Voice

Talk uses your browser’s speech recognition. That audio is handled by your browser. If Spry’s spoken replies are on, the reply text is sent to a speech service so the site can play it. The voice on/off choice is saved in this browser only.

Your chart

When you become a member after pathway education, or when Spry finishes a full intake, the site asks for your name, date of birth, sex, email, and mobile number, plus telehealth consent. That information, the problem you selected, and Spry’s education or summary go to Akute, the medical record. They are not sent back into the AI.

Membership

After intake is filed to Akute on a fast-track path, payment opens in Stripe Checkout. Join links and one-time visit links on the site also open Stripe. Stripe collects the $49 visit fee or the membership payment and keeps the billing record (name, email, card on file, charges); you can update your card or cancel a membership through the Stripe membership portal. iSpry.ai does not see or store your card number. The condition you chose on this site is never sent to Stripe; Stripe receives only your name, email, and a chart reference number so the practice can tell a membership payment from a one-time visit.

What the site itself stores

  • This browser remembers whether Spry should speak replies.
  • Your iSpry account profile (name, email, age, sex, Tennessee) for sign-in; education chat stays in the browser for that visit.
  • Pages load fonts from Google. The site does not run an advertising tracker.

Questions

Call the practice at (731) 415-0724, or read the Notice of Privacy Practices.

Analytics

The site uses Vercel Web Analytics for aggregate counts such as page views and whether someone clicked join or a one-time visit. It does not use a Meta Pixel or Google Analytics. Events do not include your name, message text, or the condition you chose.

iSpry is not for emergencies. If you think you may have a medical emergency, call 911. In crisis? Call or text 988. Spry is an AI intake assistant; it does not diagnose or prescribe. A licensed physician makes all medical decisions.
