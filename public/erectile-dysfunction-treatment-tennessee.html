Online erectile dysfunction care in Tennessee.

It is common, and it is a medical problem like any other. Adult men who are physically in Tennessee can start privately with Spry, then Dr. Merrick reviews the case.

Spry asks about your health history and the medicines you take, because some heart conditions and medicines change what is safe. A one-time visit is $49, or $100 a month covers up to 3 visits. A physician decides any prescription. Spry does not. Prescriptions and pharmacy charges are separate from the visit.

When a chat is the wrong place

  • Chest pain, shortness of breath with activity, or fainting needs an in-person evaluation first. Chest pain right now is an emergency. Call 911.
  • An erection lasting more than four hours is an emergency. Go to an emergency department.
  • Tell Spry if you take nitrates such as nitroglycerin, or have had a recent heart attack or stroke.
  • iSpry does not prescribe controlled substances, and you must be in Tennessee at the time of the visit.
