What telehealth is here

Telehealth means getting care without being in the same room as the clinician. At iSpry, Spry (an AI assistant) takes your history. A Tennessee-licensed physician, William B. Merrick, DO, or a covering clinician, then reviews it and decides the plan. The physician may message, call, or video with you, or ask for more information, before deciding. Spry does not diagnose or prescribe.

Limits and risks

  • The physician cannot examine you in person. Some problems cannot be judged safely this way, and you may be told to get in-person care instead.
  • The plan depends on what you report. Leaving out symptoms, conditions, medicines, or allergies can lead to the wrong care.
  • Technology can fail or be delayed. If you have not heard back and you are getting worse, do not wait. Seek care in person.
  • A prescription is never guaranteed. iSpry does not prescribe controlled substances.
  • No system is perfectly secure. iSpry uses safeguards described in the Notice of Privacy Practices, but electronic communication carries some privacy risk.

Not for emergencies

iSpry is not an emergency service. Call 911 for a medical emergency. Call or text 988 if you are in crisis.

Where you must be

You must be 18 or older and physically in Tennessee at the time of your visit.

Your alternatives

You can choose in-person care with your own clinician, an urgent care, or an emergency department at any time. Telehealth with iSpry does not replace a primary care clinician.

Privacy and records

Your visit is documented in a medical record kept by the practice. How your health information is used and shared, and your rights to see and correct it, are in the Notice of Privacy Practices and the privacy policy.

Fees

iSpry is direct-pay. A one-time visit is $49 and membership is $100 a month, as described in the terms. iSpry does not bill insurance or Medicare and does not enroll Medicare beneficiaries. Prescriptions, labs, and pharmacy charges are separate.

Your right to say no

Consent is voluntary. You can withdraw it at any time by calling or texting (731) 415-0724. Withdrawing does not affect care already given, and it will not affect your ability to get care elsewhere.

What checking the box means

By checking the consent box you confirm that you have read this page, understand it, have had the chance to ask questions, and agree to receive care by telehealth from William B. Merrick, DO, PLLC (iSpry). The date, time, and version of this consent are recorded in your chart.
