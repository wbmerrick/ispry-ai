Questions

What everyone asks first.

What does it cost, and what's included?
A one-time visit is $49 for a single problem, with no renewal. Membership is $100 a month, billed monthly, cancel any time, and covers up to 3 visits a month and up to 3 problems per visit. Either way you get Spry intake and physician review. You can upgrade from a one-time visit to membership whenever you want ongoing care. Prescriptions, labs, and pharmacy charges are separate and paid wherever you fill or draw them.
How fast will a doctor look at my case?
If you submit by 6 pm Central, Dr. Merrick reviews it the same day. After 6 pm, review is the next morning. You'll hear back by the phone number or email you gave us. iSpry is not for emergencies — if something can't wait, call 911 or go to the nearest ER.
What won't you prescribe?
No controlled substances — no opioids, benzodiazepines, or stimulants — through iSpry. A licensed physician decides every prescription; nothing is automatic, and Spry never prescribes anything on its own.
Do I have to be in Tennessee?
Yes — physically in Tennessee at the time of your visit. Dr. Merrick is licensed in Tennessee, and that's where the law lets him treat you. You don't have to live here; you have to be here when we see you.
What if it turns out iSpry isn't for me?
Cancel any time from Manage membership — it takes effect at the end of your billing month, with no partial-month refunds. If Spry's intake shows your problem needs in-person care, we'll say so before you ever pay.
Is what I type private?
Your chat with Spry is not stored as a medical record and is never your chart. When you pay for a visit or become a member, your name, contact details, and Spry's summary go into Akute, our HIPAA-compliant chart. Stripe handles payment and never sees your health details. The site does not use a Meta Pixel or Google Analytics. The Notice of Privacy Practices has the full picture.
Can I use Medicare, an HSA, or an FSA?
iSpry does not enroll Medicare beneficiaries and does not bill Medicare. If Medicare covers the care you need, use your Medicare clinician. The $49 visit and the membership are direct-pay. Many people pay them from an HSA or FSA; your plan administrator confirms that. West Tennessee employers can ask about a small-employer plan at (731) 415-0724.
