What does it cost, and what's included?
A one-time visit is $49 for a single problem, with no renewal. Membership is $100 a month, billed monthly, cancel any time, and covers up to 3 visits a month and up to 3 problems per visit. Need more than one visit this month? Membership covers up to 3 for $100. Either way you get Spry intake and physician review. Prescriptions, labs, and pharmacy charges are separate. A GLP-1 is priced and paid at whichever pharmacy you choose to have the prescription sent to, the same as every other prescription. iSpry charges only the visit or membership fee. See the Good Faith Estimate.
How fast will a doctor look at my case?
Your iSpry physician reviews requests 24/7, including nights, weekends, and holidays. iSpry is not for emergencies — if something can't wait, call 911 or go to the nearest ER.
What won't you prescribe?
No controlled substances — no opioids, benzodiazepines, or stimulants — through iSpry. A licensed physician decides every prescription; nothing is automatic, and Spry never prescribes anything on its own.
Do I have to be in Tennessee?
Yes — physically in Tennessee at the time of your visit. Your iSpry physician is licensed in Tennessee, and that's where the law lets the physician treat you. You don't have to live here; you have to be here when we see you.
What if it turns out iSpry isn't for me?
Cancel any time from Manage membership — it takes effect at the end of your billing month, with no partial-month refunds. If Spry's intake shows your problem needs in-person care, we'll say so before you ever pay. You get a full refund of the iSpry visit fee as soon as the request is found ineligible for this service, for example if your iSpry physician decides telehealth is not right for it.
Do I need an account before Ask Spry?
Yes. Create a free iSpry account first (name, email, password, age, sex, Tennessee). It is not membership and does not charge you. That unlocks Ask Spry. When you finish intake, Spry’s synopsis goes to your iSpry physician in your chart.
I forgot my password or email
Open Sign in, then use Forgot password? for a reset link, or Forgot email? if you are unsure which email is on the account. There is no separate username — your email is your login.
Is what I type private?
Your chat with Spry is not stored as a medical record and is never your chart. When you finish intake and consent, your name, contact details, and a summary of what you told Spry are filed to the chart before payment opens. If you never pay, that intake is already in the chart. The chart is built with HIPAA safeguards. We follow HIPAA privacy rules; see the Notice of Privacy Practices. Stripe handles payment and never sees your health details. The site does not use a Meta Pixel or Google Analytics.
Can I use Medicare, an HSA, or an FSA?
iSpry does not enroll Medicare beneficiaries and does not bill Medicare. If Medicare covers the care you need, use your Medicare clinician. The $49 visit and the membership are direct-pay. Many people pay them from an HSA or FSA; your plan administrator confirms that. West Tennessee employers can ask about a small-employer plan at (731) 415-0724.
