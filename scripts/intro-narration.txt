[open]
KICKER: iSpry.ai
CAPTION: Physician-led telehealth in Tennessee
This is iSpry. Physician-led telehealth for adults in Tennessee. Talk or type. Spry takes a short history. A physician decides. No waiting room.

[account]
KICKER: Account
CAPTION: A free account. Not a charge.
Create a free account first. Name, email, and a password. Your age, sex, and that you are in Tennessee. Not membership. No charge.

[turnstile]
KICKER: Behind the scenes
CAPTION: Invisible check. No puzzle.
A bot check can run invisibly while you sign up. You never see a puzzle. It only confirms a person is here.

[spry]
KICKER: Ask Spry
CAPTION: Leave name and birthdate out
Then say what is going on. A sore throat, for example. Leave your name and birthdate out of the chat. Spry asks a few questions, like a triage nurse, and you can tap an answer.

[caps]
KICKER: Behind the scenes
CAPTION: A daily limit keeps Spry available
Spry has a daily limit, so it stays available. If you reach it, the site tells you. An emergency is not a queue.

[synopsis]
KICKER: Synopsis
CAPTION: Most likely, plus other possibilities
Spry then writes a plain-language synopsis. The most likely problem, and other possibilities for your physician. Not a diagnosis. Spry does not prescribe.

[chart]
KICKER: Your chart
CAPTION: Filed in Akute, then Stripe
That synopsis is filed to your chart for Doctor Merrick. Your name and birthdate are added then, for the record, not in the chat.

[price]
KICKER: Cash pay
CAPTION: $49 visit, or $100 a month
Forty-nine dollars for one visit and one problem. Or one hundred dollars a month, up to three visits and three problems each. Stripe charges you, and never sees the symptom.

[review]
KICKER: Your doctor
CAPTION: Same day, if in by 6 pm Central
Submit by six PM Central, and Doctor Merrick reviews it the same day. After six, the next morning.

[pharmacy]
KICKER: Pharmacy
CAPTION: Only if a physician prescribes
Add your address and preferred pharmacy. A prescription is sent only if your physician writes one, and the pharmacy bills it. Nothing is guaranteed. No controlled substances.
